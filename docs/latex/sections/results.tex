\noindent\textbf{Working conclusion.}
Ungated correction fails. Val-selected abstain helps on mean MASE across seeds
(especially v2: $5/5$ beats base; \texttt{abs\_diff\_mean} wins $4/5$), but
Wilcoxon significance is unstable ($2/5$ for v2). Report seed-aggregated win
rates, not a single $p$-value. A learned gate is optional; a hard
\texttt{abs\_diff\_mean} abstain is the simpler story --- still on leaked M4.
